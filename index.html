How to use this tool

  1. Design on the left. Themes, Color, Type, Layout and Export are the tabs in the Design Options panel.
  2. See it on the right. The previews (web dashboard, mobile reading, social post, worksheet and more) update as you change things.
  3. Check it. The Health pill flags contrast and layout problems, with a button that takes you to each one.
  4. Take it with you. Export tokens as CSS, JSON, Tailwind or SCSS, or copy a view-only link. The Playgrounds menu has focused experiments.

Your work is saved in this browser only. Ctrl+Z undoes an edit.

Print specification

Exact values behind this worksheet. Sizes are in pt for print (A4, 1 pt = 1.333 px on screen).

An interface built only from the color roles. Light and Dark are separate palettes, shown together. The outlined one is the palette you are editing.

Card padding, the gaps between parts and the gap between cards use only the space names. Change the base unit in the left panel and everything resizes together.

Card paddingGap

Columns, gutter and margin come from the Layout grid section in the left panel. Gutter and margin use the space names. Red bands are columns, hatched blue is the margin.

Drag the right edge of the frame (or focus it and use the arrow keys) from 240 to 900 px. Fluid keeps one arrangement and the cards flex. Adaptive uses fixed 160 px cards that jump at your breakpoints. Combination flexes within a breakpoint and reflows between them. Set the mode and breakpoints in the left panel.

Checking…

    The real dashboard and reading screens, rendered off-screen at each width with your current strategy and breakpoints. Overflow fails. Text under 14 px and tap targets under 44 px warn, and fail below 12 px and 24 px. Reading line length passes at 45 to 75 characters (32 to 60 on phones under 480 px). "Long words" swaps in long Portuguese words to see whether the layout holds.
