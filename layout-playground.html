Column grid

Set the columns, the gutter between them and the outer margin. Gutter and margin are chosen from the space names, so they follow the base unit. Red bands are columns; hatched blue areas are the margins. Blocks span whole columns and snap to the bands.

Responsive strategies

Drag the right edge of the frame (or focus it and use the arrow keys) between 240 and 900 px. Fluid keeps one arrangement and lets cards flex. Adaptive uses fixed 160 px cards that jump to a new column count at your breakpoints. Combination flexes cards inside a breakpoint and reflows between them.
